Open QHPC Software Ecosystem

An open, modular software ecosystem for integrating HPC and quantum computing with vendor-neutral interfaces

Open Quantum-HPC Software Ecosystem (openQSE) is developing an open, modular ecosystem to define specifications that enable seamless integration between high-performance computing (HPC) and quantum computing through interoperable, vendor-neutral technologies. By bringing together leaders from industry, academia, and research, openQSE seeks to establish a community-driven foundation for the next generation of quantum–HPC software.

How we count

Commits reachable from each configured main branch since July 1, 2026, using the commit date. DEFw uses its master branch. Merge commits are included. Automated commits are included in the totals. Charts show eight weeks of activity in seven-day groups. The last-seven-days count includes today (UTC).

People are identified from commit authors and co-author credits; bot accounts are omitted from the people list. A shared commit can credit more than one person. Unlinked identities may appear separately until reconciled. Repository totals can overlap when histories share commits. Institution acknowledgements are curated from our community credits, rather than inferred from email addresses or tied to this date window.
