SC26 BoF: OpenQSE: Building an Open Quantum-HPC Software Ecosystem

Open items

  • Develop the final agenda and participation materials.
  • The Control Electronics Working Group is not included in this session's planned speaker lineup.

Overview

Quantum computing resources are increasingly integrated into HPC and cloud environments, but software stacks remain isolated, vendor-specific, and often proprietary. OpenQSE is a community effort developing an open, modular ecosystem of vendor-neutral specifications to close the interoperability gaps that fragment hybrid quantum-classical workflows.

This BoF will briefly share the initiative's roadmap and working-group progress, then open the floor for structured discussion with HPC facility staff, application teams, vendors, and researchers to identify priorities and shape the direction together. The intended outcome is a prioritized set of integration artifacts the community identifies as most urgent for near-term development.
