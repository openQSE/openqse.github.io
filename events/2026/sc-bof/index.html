Overview

Quantum computing resources are increasingly integrated into HPC and cloud environments, but software stacks remain isolated, vendor-specific, and often proprietary. OpenQSE is a community effort developing an open, modular ecosystem of vendor-neutral specifications to close the interoperability gaps that fragment hybrid quantum-classical workflows.

This BoF will briefly share the initiative's roadmap and working-group progress, then open the floor for structured discussion with HPC facility staff, application teams, vendors, and researchers to identify priorities and shape the direction together. The intended outcome is a prioritized set of integration artifacts the community identifies as most urgent for near-term development. We are using the openQSE demonstration effort focused on preparing a demo for SC26 to guide the BoF discussion and encourage interaction across the community and working groups.
